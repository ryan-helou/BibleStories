**A calm website that helps parents read Bible stories aloud to their children at bedtime.**

The parent is the one using the site; the kids listen, often while falling asleep. The pages use a dark, low-glare theme and large text so they are easy to read in a dim room. There are two ways to use it: read a story from a small built-in library, or ask the site to write a new story for tonight using AI (a computer program that writes text).

## What you can do with it

- Read a story from the library, each written in three versions for ages 3–5, 6–8 and 9–12.
- Describe a story or lesson you want (for example, "Daniel's courage"), pick your child's age and how many minutes you have, and get a new story written for you on the spot, along with a few things to remember and a related Bible verse.
- Have the device read any story aloud, with a choice of voice and speed and a sleep timer that stops on its own.
- Save stories you generated so you can read them again on another night.
- Pick up where you left off, since the site remembers your last story and age choice on your device.

The library is still small (three stories so far). Nothing requires an account; your progress stays in your own browser.
